MODERN EUROPEAN AND NORDIC BANK LOGOS
Collected 3 October 2026

Curated selection of major, widely known European banks and consumer bank brands, with dedicated Norway, Finland, Sweden and Denmark folders. Includes the UK, Switzerland and Iceland; Europe is not limited to the EU. Country grouping follows brand home market/headquarters rather than every market served; Nordea is grouped under Finland. Popularity is editorial selection, not a ranked popularity survey.

Current official website assets are preferred. BBVA and BNP Paribas vector files are sourced from Wikimedia, with their source pages recorded. No historical logo collections or placeholders are included. One original-format logo per brand. Logos may be wordmarks, symbols or white variants on transparent backgrounds. sources.csv records every included asset and unavailable bank. Logos remain trademarks of their respective owners; consult their brand usage terms. Assets currently served by websites may differ between local sites and parent groups.
